## 1. Summary of the Role & Responsibilities

### Role Summary
As a Program Analyst within Requirements Development and Management (RDM) under Strategic Investment Management (SIM), the incumbent evaluates requests from Veterans Health Administration (VHA) customers regarding enhancements to existing health information technology (IT) solutions or the creation of new solutions. The position bridges the gap between clinical/business operations and IT implementation across enterprise-level healthcare initiatives.

### Key Responsibilities & Major Duties
* **Requirements Development & Lifecycle Management:** Develop, manage, and trace business and functional requirements adhering to the Business Requirements Management Plan, Quality Management Plan, and change management governance.
* **Complex Project Oversight:** Lead the business analysis lifecycle for highly complex health IT projects across the VHA enterprise.
* **Intake & Initial Assessment:** Facilitate the initial assessment of incoming service requests for IT development and capture high-level business/clinical needs to determine request feasibility and acceptance.
* **Stakeholder & SME Engagement:** Actively solicit participation from clinical business owners, technical teams, and subject matter experts (SMEs) to define, validate, and prioritize requirements, ensuring continuous alignment throughout the development effort.
* **Evaluation & Quantitative/Qualitative Analysis:** Utilize qualitative and quantitative analytical techniques to assess the effectiveness and return on investment (ROI) of IT projects in meeting VHA, VA business owner, and SIM strategic goals.
* **Leadership Advisory:** Advise senior leadership on requirement trade-offs, program alignment, risk mitigation, and IT investment priorities.

## 2. Key Skills Required

### Technical & Domain Competencies
* **Requirements Analysis & Engineering:** Elicitation, documentation, validation, prioritization, and traceability of complex functional and non-functional requirements.
* **Clinical Informatics & Health IT:** Understanding healthcare workflows, electronic health records (EHR), and health IT architectures.
* **Analytical & Evaluative Methods:** Quantitative and qualitative data analysis, program evaluation, process mapping, and change management.
* **Information Management:** Organizing, structuring, and maintaining comprehensive requirements repositories and documentation.

### Core General Competencies
* **Customer Service & Client Orientation:** Building partnerships across clinical stakeholders, executive leadership, and technical vendors.
* **Communication & Influencing/Negotiating:** Facilitating consensus among diverse stakeholder groups with competing priorities.
* **Decision Making & Reasoning:** Sound judgment in scoping requirements, evaluating alternatives, and aligning solutions with agency policy.
* **Attention to Detail & Accountability:** High rigor in technical documentation, compliance tracking, and project execution.
* **Teamwork, Self-Management & Flexibility:** Managing multiple high-priority deliverables under tight timelines.

### Preferred Credentials
* **Business Analyst Certification:** Industry-recognized credentials such as IIBA (e.g., CBAP, CCBA) or PMI-PBA are strongly preferred.

## 3. Qualifications & Eligibility Requirements

### Time-In-Grade (TIG) Requirements
*(Applicable to current or former federal employees within the past 52 weeks)*

* **For GS-13:** 52 consecutive weeks of federal service at the **GS-12** grade level.
* *Note:* Must provide supporting SF-50(s) reflecting one full year at grade level (Award SF-50s are not accepted).

### Specialized Experience
* **GS-12 Grade Level:** Must possess at least one (1) full year of specialized experience equivalent to the GS-11 level in federal service. Examples include:
  * Conducting business analysis, gathering, and evaluating business needs.
  * Eliciting, documenting, validating, and prioritizing health IT business and clinical requirements.
* **GS-13 Grade Level:** Must possess at least one (1) full year of specialized experience equivalent to the GS-12 level in federal service. Examples include:
  * Two or more years leading complex health IT business and clinical requirements development.
  * Independently managing requirements elicitation, documentation, validation, prioritization, and traceability across enterprise-level initiatives.
  * Advising leadership and ensuring requirements align with broad organizational program goals.

### Education Requirements & Substitution
* **No Educational Substitution:** Education cannot be substituted for specialized experience at either the GS-12 or GS-13 grade levels.
